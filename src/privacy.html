Privacy designed for operational systems

GridGuard is designed to process only the information reasonably required to provide secure access, equipment-health monitoring, predictive risk intelligence and preventive maintenance support.

Data Governance
01

Scope of this policy

Services and users covered.

This policy applies to the GridGuard AI web platform, operator accounts, asset-health dashboards, predictive-maintenance features, alerts and related demonstration environments.

GridGuard is intended for authorized utility, engineering, maintenance and operational use.

02

Personal information

Operator and account information.

Name, work email and utility or organization name.
Operational role and permissions assigned to the account.
Authentication, session and security-event information where applicable.
Technical diagnostics required to operate or troubleshoot the platform.
03

Grid, asset and operational information

Data used to understand equipment health.

Depending on the deployment, GridGuard may receive information from transformers, substations, connected monitoring systems or authorized operator input.

Asset Equipment identity

Asset ID, equipment class, location reference and operating state.

Sensors Health telemetry

Temperature, vibration, oil condition, current and similar signals.

History Maintenance records

Inspection findings, historical incidents, alarms and maintenance activity.

Context Weather information

Rain, temperature, wind and other environmental risk factors.

✓
Operational information supplied by a utility remains that organization's underlying operational data. Processing information in GridGuard does not itself transfer ownership of that data.
04

How information is used

GridGuard operational purposes.

Authenticate authorized operators.
Monitor current and historical asset health.
Detect abnormal equipment patterns.
Rank assets according to operational risk.
Recommend inspection and maintenance priorities.
05

Sharing and disclosure

Limited authorized access.

GridGuard does not sell operator personal information or utility asset telemetry for advertising purposes.

Authorized users within the responsible organization.
Infrastructure providers required to operate the service.
Disclosure required by applicable law or valid legal process.
06

AI and predictive processing

How equipment-risk intelligence is generated.

Predictive decision support

GridGuard may use statistical or machine learning techniques to identify unusual equipment behaviour, estimate failure risk, identify high-impact assets and generate maintenance recommendations.

These outputs support human operators and engineers. They are not guarantees of equipment failure or safe operation.

Human engineering judgement remains required.
Predictions should be verified before critical operational action.
Customer-specific operational data should not be reused for unrelated model training without authorization.
07

Security

Protecting operational access.

Operator authentication and session controls.
Role-based access where configured.
Protection of application secrets and database credentials.
Audit and technical monitoring where required.
!
No internet-based platform can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their credentials and report suspected compromise.
08

Retention and deletion

Keeping information only as needed.

Information should be retained only while reasonably necessary for platform operation, security, customer requirements, historical analysis or applicable legal obligations.

Demo environments may contain temporary data that is removed when the environment is reset.

09

Your privacy choices

Account information and requests.

Request correction of inaccurate profile information.
Request account deactivation or deletion where applicable.
Ask questions about how account information is processed.
10

Browser storage

Sessions and prototype behaviour.

GridGuard may use cookies, session storage or similar technologies where needed for login sessions, interface preferences and security.

The current prototype may use browser Firebase Authentication for sessions and Firestore for profiles and operational demo data. This should not be treated as a complete production authentication system.

11

Policy updates and privacy questions

Changes, requests and security concerns.

This policy may be updated as GridGuard features, infrastructure or data-handling practices evolve. The effective date and version shown at the top of this page should identify the current published version.

🔒
When contacting support regarding privacy or security, never include passwords, authentication tokens, API keys or other confidential credentials.
